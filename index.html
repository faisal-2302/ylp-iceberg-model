The behaviour never changes on its own. It changes when you know what's holding it up.

This is the Iceberg Model.

What Rahul did is what every model of behaviour starts with, and it works the same way for everyone you manage.

ABOVE THE WATER
Visible to everyone. Easy to judge.
  • Actions
  • Decisions
  • Words
  • Habits
BELOW THE WATER
Invisible. Where the cause actually lives.
  • Beliefs
  • Values
  • Fears
  • Past experiences
  • Needs
  • Assumptions

Most of it sits below. And none of it is in any order. Someone can be driven by an old experience, a fear and a need at the same time, and one is not deeper or more important than another.

This is why two people can do the exact same thing for completely different reasons, and why the same feedback lands well with one person and badly with another.

As a manager, you will only ever manage the tip unless you go looking for the rest.

You cannot see below the waterline by observing harder. You get there by asking, and by making it safe enough for the answer to come back honest.
