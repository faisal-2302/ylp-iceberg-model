## 2. Facts that unblock the builders (verbatim from source)

**MAIN** (Workbook p.14): **M**oment (which specific moment) · **A**ction (what they did, a behaviour not a trait) · **I**mpact (why it mattered) · **N**ow (don't wait). The workbook sample card runs Moment, Action, Impact, Now, then a closing thank-you line.

**Iceberg** (Workbook p.5). Above the line, what people see: Decisions, Actions, Habits, Words. Below, what drives behaviour: Beliefs, Values, Assumptions, Past experiences, Fears, Identity, Needs.

**Eisenhower key (Sales):** Do Now = 3, 7 · Schedule = 2, 4, 6 · Delegate = 1, 5 · Eliminate = none. The workbook labels the axes Important/Not Important and Urgent/Not Urgent.

**Delegation key (Sales):** Task 1 beat report L6–7 · Task 2 dealer complaint L1–2 · Task 3 distributor payment L1–2 · Task 4 meeting agenda L7.

## 4. Conflicts between the build prompt and the source — need a ruling

1. **Trust Battery bands.** The deck says **80–100 Charging / 50–80 Holding / below 50 Draining** (slide 76). The prompt says 85/50/85. §13 says to reproduce the deck's key, so I'll use the deck unless told otherwise.
2. **Check-in cadence.** The deck's journey is Day 1, Day 30, Day 90, with certification on the Day 30 and Day 90 assessments. The workbook's chart is Day 30 and Day 120. The prompt says Day 30, 60 and 90, plus Day 7 nudges. Which is canonical for `/checkin/[n]` and for the Trust Battery arc?
3. **Fairness wording.** The deck says "Am I treated fairly?". The workbook says "Am I treated with dignity and respect?". I'd use the deck's wording.
4. **IDP best practice #3.** The deck says "One goal, not six". The workbook says "SMART goals". Both fit the builder's single goal, so I'll show both.
5. **70-20-10 Social row.** The deck says 20% feedback. The workbook says "community learnings, network, feedback, coaching and mentoring". The prompt says "stretch, exposure, shadowing". All three are close. I'll use the workbook's definition plus the deck's examples.
6. **Sales-only wording.** For example, "Key dealer escalation" in the deck versus "Customer escalation" in the workbook. Function variants need the other four decks, not rewrites.
